Your Privacy Matters

At SmartNest, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and smart home services.

Information we collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Contact Number
  • Device Information
  • Smart Home Device Preferences
  • Website Usage Data

Cookies

SmartNest uses cookies to improve your browsing experience.

Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their services.
